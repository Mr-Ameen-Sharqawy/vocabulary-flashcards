# دفعة Google Flow التجريبية 001

## الغرض

اختبار ما إذا كان Google Flow يستطيع إنتاج **أصول منفصلة** لمفردات حسية واضحة ضمن طلب واحد، لا لوحة مجمعة ولا صورة تحتوي كتابة.

## النص المقترح لـ Flow

```text
Create 12 separate, individual image assets for a Primary 4 English vocabulary flashcard library. This is a batch asset request: make one separate portrait 3:4 educational cartoon image for each numbered item below. Do NOT make a collage, contact sheet, poster, worksheet, grid, or a single image containing more than one numbered concept. Treat every item as a separate downloadable asset.

Use one consistent polished 2D storybook cartoon style across all assets: friendly Egyptian primary-school context where people appear, rounded shapes, clean dark navy outlines, warm cream backgrounds, soft shadows, and accents of coral, gold, mint, and navy. Center the main subject clearly with generous empty margin. Every image must be child-safe and easy to recognize at a glance. Do not include words, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, or visible signs.

017 bear — a friendly brown bear standing in a simple green forest.
020 bin — one clean small rubbish bin.
021 board — one simple wooden board with no writing.
022 bottles — three reusable water bottles.
026 brown bread — one fresh loaf of brown whole-wheat bread.
027 brush teeth — a child brushing their teeth beside a washbasin.
029 building — a bright simple school building seen from outside.
033 butterflies — two colorful butterflies above simple flowers.
035 cage — one small clean empty bird cage.
036 Cairo Tower — Cairo Tower clearly visible in a simple sunny city scene.
038 cap — one simple blue cap.
042 carpenter — a friendly carpenter making a small wooden chair.
```
