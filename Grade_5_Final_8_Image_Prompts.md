# Grade 5 — final 8 image prompts

Use **one prompt per image**. Every prompt is designed for a 16:9 flashcard image and explicitly excludes visible text, letters, watermarks, logos, captions, labels, speech bubbles, and borders.

## 1. affect — يؤثر على

**Suggested filename:** `affect.jpeg`

```text
Create a clean child-friendly 3D storybook cartoon illustration for an English Grade 5 vocabulary flashcard. A smiling child gently waters one small plant in a garden; the watered plant is growing tall and green while a nearby dry plant looks droopy, clearly showing that water can affect how a plant grows. Bright sunny day, simple garden background, expressive characters, warm educational mood, landscape 16:9 composition. No writing, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no border.
```
